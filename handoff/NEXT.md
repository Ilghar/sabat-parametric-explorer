# SÁBÁT: what's left from the original vision

1. **Mosaic pattern families:** move beyond the single three-pointed star to more pattern families. Use 5- and 6-fold rosettes, girih tile sets, and patterns inspired by the ceiling of the Ali Qapu music room, Isfahan (c. 1615, under Shah ʿAbbas I) (source: Babaie, “Isfahan x. Palaces”, Encyclopaedia Iranica, https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/isfahan-x2-palaces/), all built from first principles.
2. **Acoustics:** replace the heuristic proxy with a real model. Options are ray or image-source sampling, or measurements taken on the built pavilion. Then balance stability against acoustic harmony.
3. **Connector CAD:** produce parametric geometry (STL or OpenSCAD) for the three main 3D-printed and machined connector types, driven by the connector table.
4. **Textile envelope:** plan the knitted Texcycle yarn and recycled-fabric panels, from used clothes as a collection of memories. That covers per-panel area, a cutting list and a provenance and story ledger.
5. **Structural checks:** load cases, sensitivity of strut lengths, and the statistical testing mentioned in the thesis.
6. **3D view:** shade the panels, show infills in 3D, toggle the textile layer, and export.
7. **"Gateways to Heaven" study:** compare cosmological geometry across Chogha Zanbil, the Isfahan Friday Mosque, the Louvre pyramid and Notre-Dame.
8. **Space-forming configurations:** support layouts beyond a single dome, as the module system allows "various space-forming configurations".
